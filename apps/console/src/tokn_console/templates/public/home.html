What a request costs

Input, output and cached tokens are priced separately, per model, and the price that applied is written on the receipt. Nothing is rounded in our favour beyond the last micro-naira.

Reserved before

The worst case is held from your wallet before the request leaves us. If it does not fit, you get a clear 402 and nothing runs.

Measured after

The rest of the hold is released and the real cost is charged, read from the provider's own token counts.

Recorded always

Model, tokens in, out and cached, cost in naira, latency and outcome. Never the prompt, never the answer.

What we store, and what we do not

We store

Your account, your keys as hashes, your wallet and ledger, and one receipt per request: model, tokens, cost, latency, outcome.

We do not store

Prompts, completions, request bodies or authorisation headers. There is no setting to turn this back on, because there is nothing to turn on.

Questions

Is this a subscription?

No. You prepay in naira and spend it per token. Credit never expires, and there is no monthly minimum.

Which models can I use?

The catalogue lists real model names with their input, output and cache prices. If a model is not listed, it is not enabled.

What happens if my balance runs out mid-request?

It cannot. The worst case is held before the request starts, so a request either fits or is refused with a 402 before anything runs.

Can I cap what a key spends?

Yes. Each key can carry a spend cap and an expiry. When the cap is reached the key stops working; it does not overspend.

Do you use my prompts to train anything?

No. We do not store them, so there is nothing to train on.

What if a request fails upstream?

You are not charged. The hold is released in full, and the failed attempt is recorded with an upstream error outcome.
