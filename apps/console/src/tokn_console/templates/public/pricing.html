Pricing

Pay for the tokens you use.

No subscription, no monthly minimum, no expiry. You prepay in naira, every request is priced per token against the catalogue, and the price that applied is written on the receipt.

Priced per model

Input, output and cached tokens carry separate prices. A cheaper model is cheaper per token, not cheaper per month.

Reserved, then measured

The worst case is held before the request runs and the real cost is charged after. If it does not fit, nothing runs.

Credit never expires

What you top up stays yours. It is a balance, not a bucket that empties on the first of the month.

Questions

Is there a free tier?

No. Free credit attracts abuse and we would rather be honest than generous on paper.

What happens when a key reaches its cap?

It stops accepting requests. The wallet is untouched and the cap can be raised.

Do failed requests cost anything?

No. If the upstream fails before any content arrives, the hold is released in full.

How do I pay?

By card through Paystack, in naira. Every payment produces a receipt.
