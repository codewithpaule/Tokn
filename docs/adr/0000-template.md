# NNNN. Short title in the imperative mood

- Status: proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR-NNNN
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Deciders: names

## Context

What is the situation? What forces are at play — constraints from the product, the
threat model, the team, the budget? State the facts, not the conclusion.

## Decision

What we will do, in one or two sentences, in the active voice: "We will ...".

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what we are deliberately giving up. List
the follow-up work this creates, and the conditions under which we would revisit it.
