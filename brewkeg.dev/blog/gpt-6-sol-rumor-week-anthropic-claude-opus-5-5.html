GPT 6 Sol is supposedly dropping this week. Here is what is actually known.

OpenAI and Anthropic are rumored to ship new flagships within hours of each other, with at least one personal agent platform following before DevDay on September 29. A clean read of what is rumor, what is leak, and what is shipping.

2026-09-22·#openai#anthropic#gpt-6#claude-opus-5-5#leaks

This week is going to be one of those weeks.

If the rumor mill is half right, by Friday we will have a new GPT 6 Sol from OpenAI, a new Claude Opus 5.5 from Anthropic, the end of the Haiku line, at least one new personal agent platform, and an OpenAI DevDay on September 29 where the company will try to define what "an always on AI teammate" actually means in product form. Polymarket currently prices Opus 5.5 at a 75 percent chance of releasing within 24 hours of this post going up.

Most of what follows is not confirmed. Some of it will be wrong by the time you read it. The point of this post is to lay out what is rumor, what is leak, what is shipping, and what brewkeg will do when the dust settles.

What is rumored

The most circulated rumor on developer Twitter this week comes from Chubby at kimmonismus, who has been the most consistently correct leaker on model release windows for the past year. The summary, paraphrased from his timeline:

  • GPT 6 Sol is expected to ship on Tuesday (US time), pitched as a jump over GPT 6 Astra
  • Anthropic is rumored to counter with Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 on the same day or the next
  • Claude Haiku is rumored to be retired, alongside GPT Terra
  • OpenAI's "Grok Bot" (code named Aeon) is expected before DevDay on September 29, possibly this week
  • A new pretrained model at OpenAI called Bel is already trained and ready as a response to Anthropic's Fable

That last one is worth pausing on. "Bel" is the rumored next pretraining after Doug, which is what Astra was based on. If Bel is real and trained, then GPT 6 Sol is not the model OpenAI is most worried about. It is the placeholder while the real fight happens between Bel and Anthropic's Fable.

The other thing circulating is a roadmap image from Pankaj Kumar listing what every major lab is expected to ship this fall. The notable entries:

  • Anthropic: Opus 5.5, Fable 5.2 (may become 5.5)
  • OpenAI: GPT 6 Sol, GPT Terra (both could come tomorrow)
  • Google: Gemini 4 Pro, Gemini 4 Flash
  • Moonshot: Kimi K3.1
  • Z.ai: GLM 5.5 (skipping 5.4 in China, which is a tradition at this point)
  • DeepSeek: V5 (not in September, October most likely)
  • Qwen: Qwen 4 Max, Qwen 4 Flash, Qwen 4 Plus

The roadmap also explicitly says Luna is not coming, and Astra 6.1 is being prepared but will not release soon.

What is shipping

Of everything in the rumor pile, the one thing that is genuinely confirmed is that OpenAI removed the Ultrafast service tier from gpt 5.6 sol in Codex this week. Ultrafast is the tier that pushes GPT 5.6 Sol up to 750 output tokens per second on Cerebras hardware, roughly 14 times the standard speed. The removed entry in Codex literally described it as "the fastest available responses for latency sensitive work."

You do not pull your fastest tier off an old model right before a DevDay unless you have something to replace it with. The most plausible read: GPT 6 Sol ships with Ultrafast as the default positioning. The 750 tokens a second number is what OpenAI wants developers to associate with the new flagship.

The agent side is more concrete. OpenAI has reportedly hired Peter Steinberger, the founder of the open source agent framework OpenClaw, from Meta to lead next generation personal agent development. The framework is already public. The team is in place. The product (Aeon) is rumored to ship before DevDay. The three way race between SpaceXAI's Grok Bot (shipped August 11), Meta's Muse (shipped September 8), and OpenAI's Aeon (shipped imminently) is real, and brewkeg will be routing to all of them as fast as their APIs stabilize.

What brewkeg does next

When these models ship, here is what happens on our side:

  1. The brewkeg proxy will pull the new Anthropic and OpenAI model identifiers from /v1/models as soon as they appear on the upstream endpoints. That happens automatically within minutes of launch, no manual config on our end.
  2. The /compare surface picks them up the next time the AA cache refreshes. New slugs appear in the picker within 24 hours of launch.
  3. The pricing in brewkeg's catalog updates from upstream list price. If the Opus 5.5 pricing leak is right and Anthropic cuts the price meaningfully, that flows straight through to your bill.

The honest version: if you are building on brewkeg today and your code calls model: "claude opus 5", you do not need to change anything. The next time you deploy against the proxy, the new models are available as claude opus 5 5, gpt 6 sol, claude sonnet 5 5, etc., and the picker on the dashboard will surface them with their real numbers.

We will post a follow up here once the dust has settled, with concrete benchmark numbers from brewkeg's own evaluation runs against whatever actually ships. Subscribe via RSS if you want the comparison data the day it lands.
