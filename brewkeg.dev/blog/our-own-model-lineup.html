We can't keep selling you Opus at ₦7,000 ($5), so we cut a deal to keep it yours

Reselling Opus at ₦7,000 ($5) was going to break us. So we signed a new deal: the same Opus-class brain under our own names, pay as you go, credit that never expires.

2026-11-08·#launch#models#gateway

Two months ago we started brewkeg with one simple goal: sell good models cheap. For a while it worked, and if you were here from day one you got the real thing, real Opus and real Sonnet, straight.

Then our provider ran the numbers and it was not working for them. At our prices it was not working for us either. There is no big AI datacentre in Nigeria, so cheap local power was never on the table, and every token travels far before it lands in your terminal in Lagos or Abuja. We signed a new deal with a new provider to keep the lights on. Here is the funny part: if you used brewkeg last week, you already ran on the new rails, quietly, on the same endpoint with the same setup, and the work still got done.

But the new maths looks like the old maths, and we cannot keep selling premium Opus type models at the same price, not at ₦27,500 ($20) a tier while keeping quotas kind. So instead of giving you less in secret and hoping you will not notice, we signed a new deal with a new provider: same brain at a fair price, and that is the honest fix.

Three tiers plus the free lane. No vendor badges, no long list of GPT this and Claude that, just three names that are easy to pick, plus free that costs you nothing:

Why white-label at all

Because you should be building, not tracking model names.

Vendor names change every quarter: a new version drops, the old one gets retired, your config breaks, and suddenly your afternoon is changelogs instead of code. Ultra, Pro, and Nano do not move. The brain behind them can get better without your setup noticing. You pick a tier once, max brain, daily work, or fast and cheap, and you stay in your editor while we do the chasing.

That is the whole point of the lineup: no version roulette, no renaming your models every launch cycle, just build.

Why we are telling you this

If you run a startup in Nigeria, you know how the maths goes here: light, bandwidth, everything priced in dollars. Our promise has not moved: cheap access with no monthly quota that punishes you. A sub that kills your unused tokens is a bad deal for the week you travel. Metered credit that never expires, that is the whole product, and that part stays.

What changes is only the name you pick. We started by serving the right models under their own names, and now our new provider deal gives us the same power under simple names: fair price, built to last, no quiet downgrade, and no hoping you will not check. This post is us telling you straight.

What you need to do

Almost nothing. All your setup keeps working, and model names in Claude Code, Codex, and OpenCode still resolve. You will see Ultra, Pro, and Nano in the picker, on the pricing page, and on your dashboard, while the old names keep working behind them. Try the new tiers when they land. If they do the work, stay. Early users like you are why this exists. If they do not, we will help you move. No hard feelings.

That is the story: two months, one failed bet on reselling, one new deal, and a lineup with our own name on it. Onwards.
