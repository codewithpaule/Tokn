Privacy

This policy explains what brewkeg collects, why, and how you can exercise your rights. brewkeg is an AI inference gateway: your requests are routed to the model provider you select and billed by the token. We are the billing and routing layer, not the model.

What we collect

  • Account data. Your email address and account identifier, via our authentication provider. Authentication sessions use cookies.
  • API keys. The keys you mint for your own use. Keys are stored so the gateway can authenticate your requests.
  • Usage and billing data.For every request we record the model, provider, timestamp, request count, status, and input/output/cache token counts, so we can meter and invoice you accurately.
  • Request and response content. By default we store the full content of each request and response — the system prompt, messages, tool definitions, the model’s response, its reasoning, and tool calls — together with the usage metadata above. We use this to operate and support the service, diagnose errors, and prevent abuse. It is configurable: the gateway can be operated with content logging disabled, in which case only the token and billing metadata above is kept. The in-app Data protection page shows the current setting.
  • Payment records. Transaction references, amounts, and status from our payment processor. We never see or store your full card or bank details. A successful payment grants usage credit in your account; it is not held as a cash balance and is not refundable. We retain the transaction record for accounting and fraud-prevention purposes after your account closes, and we delete the usage and request detail behind it — see Retention below.
  • Optional contact and referrals. If you opt in, a phone number for product messages (WhatsApp). We record your opt-in choice and timestamp. If you use a referral link, we record that you were referred and by whom.
  • Analytics. We use privacy-conscious web analytics and advertising pixels (see Cookies below) to measure how the site and signup flow are used.

How we use data

To operate the gateway and route your requests; to meter, bill, and support you; to keep the service secure and prevent abuse; to communicate with you if you ask us to; and to understand aggregate product usage. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your request content to train third-party models.

Model providers and subprocessors

To answer a request, we forward it to the model provider for the model you selected (for example Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google), which processes it under its own terms and data-retention policies. We also rely on sub-processors for hosting and databases (Supabase), payments (Paystack), and transactional email (Sendly). The current list is on the support page.

Cookies and analytics

We set cookies and run analytics and advertising scripts (Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, Meta Pixel, and the X (Twitter) Pixel) to understand traffic and measure conversions. These may set cookies in your browser. A cookie notice is shown on first visit; continuing to use the site constitutes consent. If you are in a jurisdiction that requires explicit opt-in, contact us and we will address it.

How long we keep data

Account, usage, and billing records are retained for as long as your account is active and as needed to meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations. Request and response content is retained with the usage record and deleted alongside it. We do not currently apply an automatic time-based deletion to stored content; if you want your content removed, request deletion below.

Closing your account does not entitle you to a payout of remaining credit, and deletion of your data does not create a refund — see Refunds in the terms. Financial transaction records are retained after closure for the statutory accounting period; the usage content behind them is deleted on request.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to complain to a data-protection authority. To exercise any of these, email brewkegsupport@gmail.com from the email on your account. We will respond within a reasonable period. You can also request deletion of your account and all associated data.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards (access controls, encryption in transit) to protect your data. No system is perfectly secure. Please do not send us secrets or regulated personal information you would not want stored.

Children

brewkeg is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

If we materially change this policy we will update the date above. For any privacy question, or to exercise your rights, email brewkegsupport@gmail.com.
