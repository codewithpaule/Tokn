What your plan includes

Every plan carries every model except the ones marked Pro Max only. Which models a plan carries is one question; how fast they eat your monthly budget is another. That is the Credit column: a 2× model spends twice the quota of a 1× one for the same call.

Asking for a model your plan doesn't carry returns a 400 model_not_in_plan before any tokens are spent, so you are never charged for it. Compare plans.

What the tiers mean

Free 0×
Costs nothing, ever. Slower than the paid tiers — this is the lane your account falls back to when its quota is spent.
Eco 0.85×
Small and fast — the cheapest way to do routine work.
Standard 1×
The default tier. Most models sit here.
Max 2×
Frontier reasoning. Burns budget quickly, so use it on the hard calls. Pro Max only.

How credit spends your quota

Each plan pre-buys a monthly token budget. Every call draws from that budget at its model's credit weight, and the budget resets on the 1st. One Opus call costs the same quota as roughly two and a half standard calls.
