Billing and payment

We bill by the token. You can pay in NGN or USD, in whichever currency your bank supports; the choice is made at signup and can be changed. The two are separate ledgers — we do not convert between them at an exchange rate, and each purchase grants usage in the currency you paid in.

  • Pay-as-you-go credit. Top-up credit does not expire. Whatever you pay for and do not spend is still yours next month, the month after, and the year after. There is no monthly reset and no expiry clock.
  • Subscriptions. Subscription plans bill on a recurring 30-day cycle and include a monthly token and request allowance. The allowance is specific to the plan and resets each cycle. A subscription and pay-as-you-go credit are different things; having one does not cancel the other.
  • Rates. Per-token rates are listed on the pricing page and are stated per 1M tokens. Rates are fixed in your billing currency and are not subject to exchange-rate swings after the purchase.
  • Payment processing. Payments are handled by our processor (Paystack). We never see or store your full card or bank details. A top-up is credited to your account once the processor confirms the payment.
  • First-funding bonus. Eligible first-time funding may include a promotional usage bonus. The bonus is discretionary, applied once, and may be withdrawn or changed at any time.
  • Refunds. We do not refund payments. Purchased credit is not cash, is not cash-equivalent, and has no expiry date precisely because it is yours to spend rather than money to be returned. Once a payment has been credited to your account, that payment has been used to buy usage capacity and is non-refundable, whether or not you have spent it. We are not obliged to refund, and we will not, for any reason other than the two below.
  • The only two situations we will refund. If we charged you more than once for a single purchase, or if a charge was taken and never delivered to your account because of our fault. In both cases email us from the address on your account and we will correct it. Nothing else qualifies.
  • Understanding the system before you buy. Rates, model availability per plan, weighted-token maths and credit behaviour are published on the pricing page, the model access page and in llms.txt. Buying without reading them does not create a refund right, and we will not refund on the basis that a product was not what you expected. Please read them first; we would rather you decline than be surprised.

Cancelling a subscription

Cancelling stops future billing. The current cycle continues to the end of the period you have already paid for, and is not prorated, partial, or refunded back to you. If you cancel on day one of a 30-day cycle, that cycle is still billed in full; cancellation is not a way to buy a shorter month. Any pay-as-you-go credit you hold is unaffected by cancelling, and remains yours under the same no-refund rule above.

Output from these models is unfiltered and may be unsuitable; do not send secrets, credentials, or regulated data through them.
