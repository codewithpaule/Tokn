The differences are volume, not permission.

Everything below is true of all four tiers and of a bare top-up, so it is stated once instead of repeated in each card.

  • Claude, GPT and open-weight models on one key — no per-vendor setup
  • Cache reads billed at 0.1×, writes at 1.25× — the longer a session runs, the more of each turn is a cheap re-read
  • Top-up credit never expires; plan quota resets each billing cycle, and switching plans moves the leftover to your wallet.
  • No overage, no surprise invoice — you stop when the credit stops
  • One API, Anthropic format, works with Claude Code, Codex CLI and OpenCode
