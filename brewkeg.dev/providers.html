Routing and settlement

We handle the business side.

How routing works

Our edge checks quota, stamps each request, and forwards to the healthiest pool. If your pool caps, traffic fails over automatically.

Reliability target

99.5% monthly availability per pool is the target we design around — confirmed per endpoint, never promised up front.

Economics

Payouts follow weighted-credit tiers (eco 0.5× · std 1× · max 2×). Your final rate is confirmed once your endpoint is live and settling.

What we handle

Customer acquisition, billing, key management, quota, abuse, and support. You keep the hardware running.
