Know before you enable

What you are opting into

  • No moderation

    Output can include anything. No filters, no refusals, no appeals process.

  • Your prompts are visible upstream

    Content you send may be logged or analysed by the model provider. Do not send secrets, credentials, or client data.

  • Every token costs double

    Uncensored routes bill at 2× credit on top of the model's tier rate. The surcharge is deliberate — it prices out casual use.

  • Off until you say otherwise

    Requests to uncensored-* return 403 until you flip the toggle. Turning it off takes effect on the next request.
